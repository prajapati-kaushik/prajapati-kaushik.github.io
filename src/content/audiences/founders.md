## Why this might be you

You already have a product that customers pay for. It runs on PHP, or on an API-first stack where PHP is one of the pieces. The codebase is older than you would like, the person who wrote it has moved on, and every new feature feels riskier than the last. You need someone who can take the PHP side off your plate — APIs, data, and the templates that render them — without stopping the business.

## What I have done that looks like your problem

- Kept a Dutch parent portal used by millions of new parents available while its backend was replaced. Purchases still turned into access, intake and pregnancy-week email still arrived, and the API stayed compatible for the apps already using it. [Read the case](/work/zwangerenportaal/).
- Kept payments and invoices consistent while a parent buys something — the paid record is never silently rewritten, and the activation step knows what was bought.
- Moved older PHP products — mail, alerting, internal tools — onto Symfony without a shutdown. A parenting product's email kept sending after its old provider was replaced.
- Added small integrations the business needed quickly: an invoice-generation endpoint for the finance workflow, a webhook flow for account changes, with the request and the note in the same place.

## What the first month is for

1. A written map of the system as it is: what calls what, where money and email move, what is fragile.
2. The first slice shipped behind a compatible interface, so you can see the method before you commit to the work.
3. Root-cause write-ups for whatever is currently hurting, with the fix and the query attached.
4. A plan in plain language that you, not only another engineer, can read and challenge.

## How I work with founders

- **Business first.** I ask what the business needs before I ask what the code needs.
- **Small increments.** I ship in small, tested steps. You see progress weekly, not at the end of a quarter.
- **Written down.** Decisions, runbooks, and handovers live in the repo, not in my head.
- **AI, reviewed.** I use AI tooling where it speeds up analysis and migration drafts, and I review everything it produces.

## Fit

**Good fit:** a product people already pay for, on PHP/Symfony or with PHP as one of the pieces. Healthtech or tools especially. One engineer owns a domain: API, data, templates, deploy. Contract, B2B, or a long-term role.

**Not a fit:** "move fast and skip the tests", meeting-heavy founder-led standups, US Pacific-only overlap, or a hire whose job is to abandon PHP in week one.
